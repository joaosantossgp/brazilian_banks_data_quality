## Provenance and fidelity

Original official browser CSVs are saved at `data/raw/discovery-20261001/portal-summary-201012.original.csv` (334,931 bytes) and `portal-summary-202412.original.csv` (282,664 bytes). Native UTF-8 table captures, generated-evidence manifests, original HTTP metadata/catalog/cadastro and numeric input bodies are retained with URL, parameters, retrieval/archive time, status, diagnostics and SHA-256. Browser-generated CSV/table manifests deliberately have no invented HTTP status. Original saved-file capture timestamps are recorded separately from subsequent archival times.

The official [BCB portal](https://www3.bcb.gov.br/ifdata/index2024.html) labels monetary CSV fields **R$ mil** and formats/rounds them. Inventory instead preserves the official table's unformatted monetary values, labeled `BRL_unformatted_portal_table`, alongside the exact CSV token. Agency/attendance counts retain their source units. Missing markers are checked against formatting; no NA/NI-to-zero substitution is permitted. In these two actual captures the 234 NI cells agree with the CSV. Fixture tests cover the formatting mismatch case.

The portal display-code namespace is not asserted to equal OData CodInst or full CNPJ. Banco do Brasil's observed display code `0` remains `0`. Columns are validated against the selected official report metadata, both CSV headers and table row ordering. The source's shared quarterly numeric shard includes other reports/types; it is archived only as provenance, while accepted observations are restricted to individual Resumo.

The independent `upstream-cell-audit.csv` compares all cells that are stored directly under the report's definition key in the official numeric input. All 9,720 direct cells in 2010 and 7,890 in 2024 match. Other fields can be computed by the portal (profit), drawn from registration (agency counts), or represented by portal missing markers. Absence of a direct numeric key is therefore recorded as an unresolved source mechanism, never financial missingness. The 2,685 blanks are **observed portal-table blanks**; their underlying interpretation requires registration/reporting semantics and is not silently equated to zero or JSON null.

There are eight variables each quarter and nine distinct raw definition keys across the two quarters. `Lucro Líquido` uses `portal_ifd:79698` in 2010 and `portal_ifd:79718` in 2024. `structural-presence.csv` records absence of these specific definition IDs in the other report, **not absence of profit or a proven economically comparable definition**. Definition reconciliation remains future work. Portal historical reports may be revised retrospectively; these are retrieval-time snapshots, not asserted contemporaneous publication vintages.

## OData failure investigation

Service root, official metadata and `ListaDeRelatorio()` returned 200; individual cadastro and Resumo value requests returned 500, with the retained generic 60-byte `Erro desconhecido` body. A separate read-only diagnostic reported 11 bounded probes covering the documented alias/inline/encoded syntax, JSON/CSV, `$top=1`, types 1/3 and reports 1/T across 201012, 202412 and 202606. These returned the same SHA-256 `dbc4949b12281ca77fae10d261d4f539e633d08be0abeef5143d685056b904a9`. Its diagnostic artifacts belong to the parent task; the local archive holds this pilot's own request evidence.

Official signatures and inspected reference implementations support the constructed queries. There is no evidenced parameter fix. An internal BCB data-provider/execution failure is a leading hypothesis; exact root cause requires server logs. The whole service is not offline. The accepted pilot therefore uses the clearly labeled official portal CSV fallback, and no extra diagnosis probes or BCB contact were initiated here.

Historical uninterrupted active registration, equity listing and equity tickers remain **unknown**.
